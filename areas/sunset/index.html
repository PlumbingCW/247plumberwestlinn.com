Plumber in Sunset, West Linn, OR

Sunset is in the southeast corner of West Linn, north of Interstate 205 and the Willamette River. It mixes single-family streets with apartments and townhomes, and the ground runs from river-level lowlands up to a ridge near Summit and Knox Streets.

A neighborhood of mixed housing types

The City of West Linn neighborhood plan counted 1,055 housing units in Sunset in 2007. Of those, 802 were single-family homes, 96 were medium-density multifamily units and 115 were high-density multifamily units. The plan also counted 40 accessory dwelling units.

The mix matters for plumbing. In a house, the main shutoff and the water heater are yours. In a condo or apartment, a shared line or a building shutoff may be someone else's, so the first call may be to a manager or association.

Creeks, wetlands and low ground in Sunset

The plan describes several small creeks, the largest being Sunset Creek, and wetlands in the Camassia Natural Area. The highest point, about 620 feet, is at the northwest border near Summit and Knox Streets. The eastern and southern parts along the Willamette and Tualatin Rivers are low, about 55 to 60 feet above sea level.

The plan says parts of the neighborhood along the Willamette River lie in FEMA's 100 year flood plain. We do not know your lot, so look up your own address on the FEMA flood map or ask the City.

What this means for plumbing: a house on low ground may see water at the floor drain or in a crawlspace when rain is heavy and the ground is full. A plumber can check the drains inside the house, but groundwater itself is not a plumbing fault.

Source type: City of West Linn Sunset Neighborhood Plan, 2008

If a drain backs up in a Sunset house

Do these while you wait for the plumber.

  • Stop running water, including the washer and dishwasher.
  • Keep people and pets out of the wet area.
  • Check whether more than one fixture is affected, since that points to the main line.
  • Find the cleanout cap if you know where it is, but do not open it without a plumber.

Which line is yours

The plan says the city sanitary sewer is separate from the storm sewer, and that Water Environment Services of Clackamas County treats the wastewater. The private line runs from your house to the city main. A blockage in the private line is a plumber's job. For a problem in the street main, contact West Linn Public Works.

A plumber can use sewer line cleaning to clear the private line and sewer inspection to see it on camera.

Water in Sunset

The same plan says West Linn gets its water from the South Fork Water Board, which West Linn shares with Oregon City, and that the source is the Clackamas River. For worn fixtures or a heater that has stopped working, see water heater repair and leak detection.
