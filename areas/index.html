West Linn and the nearby towns

West Linn is the home base, at ZIP 97068. Oregon City sits just across the Willamette River, Gladstone is next to it, Lake Oswego lies north along Highway 43 and Tualatin is west by Stafford Road and I-205.

Communities such as Stafford, Rivergrove, Jennings Lodge and Canby are covered on the nearest town page.

What changes from town to town

Each town has its own water and sewer system and its own permit office, so the practical advice differs. Oregon City buys Clackamas River water treated by the South Fork Water Board. Tualatin buys soft Portland water from Bull Run. Gladstone owns its sewer pipes and sends flow to a county treatment system. Lake Oswego pipes Clackamas River water to a treatment plant in West Linn.

Our town pages explain what that means for your pipes, your water heater and your sewer line. Each page also lists the ZIP codes we serve and the steps to take before a plumber arrives.
