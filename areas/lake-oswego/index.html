Oswego Lake and the city's growth

The Oregon Encyclopedia says the town was founded in 1850 as Oswego, and that in 1960 residents chose Lake Oswego as the incorporated city's name, with the city's population and acreage nearly doubling. It also describes a 1930s building boom promoted around recreation.

Why that matters for you: a house built in the 1930s boom is now ninety years old, and its drain, supply lines and vents were laid before modern materials. Ask for a pipe condition check before a major remodel or purchase.
