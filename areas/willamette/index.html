The Willamette Historic District

What the city says about the district

The City of West Linn says the Willamette Historic District was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 2009. Its period of significance runs from 1895 to 1929. The first houses went up in 1895 and building fell off by 1929, which the city links to the Great Depression and a shrinking supply of lots.

The district has 38 officially designated historic properties and 26 noncontributing structures, which are either newer than 1929 or in styles that do not match. Exterior changes in the district need City approval before work begins.

What this means for plumbing: most plumbing is inside the walls, but a job that changes the outside of a house, such as a new vent or a visible line, may need that approval. Ask the City planning staff first, and tell the plumber what you learn.

The city page does not give the district boundaries, so we use the street address you give us rather than guess which side of a line a house sits on.

Questions to ask before work on an older house

These keep an older house from surprising you.

  • What is the supply pipe made of at the meter and at the water heater.
  • Where is the main shutoff and does the valve turn.
  • Is the drain line to the street original or has it been replaced.
  • Has a past remodel left a mix of old and new pipe.
  • Is any of the work visible from outside, which can trigger city review.

If you find a leak in an older house

Steps that limit damage until the plumber arrives.

  • Shut the main valve. If it will not turn, do not force it, and say so when you call.
  • Open the lowest tap to drain the line.
  • Turn off the water heater if it is the source.
  • Move belongings away from wet walls and floors.
  • Take a photo of the damage and call (503) 974-3329.

Plumbing jobs in older houses

Older homes often get partial updates, a new bath here and a new kitchen there. That can leave different pipe types joined together. A plumber who looks first can tell you whether the whole house needs work or only one run. See leak detection for hidden leaks and repiping when old supply pipe has reached its end.

For clogs in older drain lines, drain cleaning clears them, and a camera shows the cause. For a heater that has aged out, see water heater replacement.
