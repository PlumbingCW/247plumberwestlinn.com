Plumber in Parker Crest, West Linn, OR

Parker Crest is a central West Linn neighborhood of more than 500 homes between Rosemont Road, Salamo Road and Summit Street. It is made up of several subdivisions, which the neighborhood plan names.

Boundaries and subdivisions

What the neighborhood plan documents

The Parker Crest Neighborhood Plan, adopted by the City Council in 2007, puts the boundaries at Summit Street to the east, Rosemont Road to the north, Salamo Road to the west and generally Sabo Lane to the south. It says the neighborhood has over 500 residences, along with home-based businesses, public facilities and open space.

Subdivisions named in the plan are Hoodview, McKenzie Rose, Rogerfield, Parker Summit and Rosemont Summit. The plan gives no ages or house types, so we do not.

What this means for plumbing: the plan, written around 2006, says some streets lacked storm drainage and had standing water at the roadside, and that several sewer segments needed upgrades. Those are city systems, so they say nothing about the pipes inside your house.

Source type: City of West Linn Parker Crest Neighborhood Plan

City side and house side

When a drain backs up, the first question is where the blockage is. If only one fixture is slow, the problem is in the house branch. If several fixtures back up together, the problem may be in the private line to the street or in the city main.

A plumber can clear and inspect the private line with sewer line cleaning and sewer inspection. For the street main, contact West Linn Public Works.

Before the plumber arrives at a backed-up Parker Crest home

Small steps that limit the damage.

  • Stop using sinks, tubs, showers and toilets.
  • Keep the washer and dishwasher off.
  • Move rugs and boxes off the floor near the lowest drain.
  • Note which fixtures are affected and tell the dispatcher.
