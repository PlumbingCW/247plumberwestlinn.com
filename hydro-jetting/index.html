Emergency Hydro Jetting in West Linn, OR

Hydro jetting uses pressurized water from a hose and nozzle to scrub the inside of a drain or sewer line clean. When a cable machine only punches a hole through the blockage and the clog returns, jetting removes the buildup instead of moving it.

If a line is backing up now, stop running water and call (503) 974-3329. Hydro jetting emergencies are handled 24/7, with a camera check first to make sure the pipe can take the pressure.

When a backup needs hydro jetting

Jetting is the right tool when a line keeps clogging after cabling, when grease has coated the pipe walls, or when fine roots have formed a mat that a cable cannot remove. In those cases the line empties for a few weeks, then slows again. The cable cleared a path, but the pipe is still narrow.

A full backup is the urgent case. When several fixtures are affected, the plumber may cable first to restore flow, then schedule jetting as the follow-up. If the line is open but the problem keeps returning, jetting is the lasting fix.

While you wait for a hydro jetting visit

Preparation makes access easier and limits mess.

  • Stop using water so the line does not keep filling.
  • Locate the cleanout, usually a capped pipe near the foundation, and clear a path to it.
  • Move belongings away from the lowest drain in case of overflow.
  • Note how long the problem has been returning.

What jetting removes and what it cannot

Jetting removes grease, soap scum, silt, scale and root fibers. It cleans the pipe wall and flushes debris out to the main. It cannot repair a collapsed pipe, an offset joint or a crushed section, and it should never be used on a pipe that is already structurally failing.

That is why a camera inspection comes first. A sewer camera inspection confirms the pipe is sound enough, and a second pass afterward shows how clean the line is. If the camera finds damage, the plan moves to sewer line repair.

Grease, restaurants and commercial lines

Restaurants and food service shops produce a lot of grease, and it builds in kitchen lines and grease interceptors. Regular jetting is part of a maintenance schedule for those properties. Grease trap cleaning is the related service, and it keeps the interceptor itself working.

Storm drain and yard drain cleaning

Roof drains, area drains and storm drains clog with leaves, silt and needles, and heavy autumn rain exposes it. A jetter flushes these lines and clears debris that has settled in low spots. If water ponds near a foundation after rain, a clogged drain is the first thing to check.

The plumber confirms where the line discharges and whether it connects to the sanitary system, because that affects what can be done.
