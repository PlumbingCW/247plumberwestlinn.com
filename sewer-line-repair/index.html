Sewer backup: what to do first

If sewage is coming up from a floor drain, a tub or a toilet, treat it as a health problem as well as a plumbing problem. Stop running water, keep children and pets away, and avoid touching the water with bare hands. Open windows if the smell is strong.

Do not use chemical cleaners on a main line backup. They do not clear a failed pipe, and they put caustic liquid in front of the technician. If the backup happened after heavy rain, the cause may be the municipal line rather than your lateral, and the plumber can tell the difference with a camera.

What causes sewer line damage

The usual causes are root intrusion, bellied sections that hold water, cracked or collapsed pipe, offset joints and old pipe material that has reached the end of its life. Roots enter at joints and cracks in search of moisture, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease.

Cast iron lines rust from the inside and flake, which narrows the pipe and eventually breaks it. Clay tile lines come apart at joints. Fiber conduit known as Orangeburg softens and deforms. A camera inspection shows which material you have and what shape it is in.

Sewer backup is an emergency when

Sewage is rising in a tub or floor drain, several fixtures back up together, you smell sewage inside, or water is standing in a basement. Stop water use and call the 24/7 line. If anyone has contact with sewage, wash thoroughly and keep wounds covered.

Trenchless pipe lining and when it works

Pipe lining is a repair for lines that are damaged but still hold their shape. A resin-soaked liner is pulled into the pipe and cured to form a new pipe inside the old one. It avoids trenching through landscaping and driveways and can be faster than excavation.

It is not right for every pipe. A collapsed section or a line with significant bellies will not line well, and the camera decides. We describe the camera findings plainly before recommending a repair, and we are clear when digging is the better choice.

Cast iron sewer pipe repair

Cast iron drains and sewer sections in older homes often fail at the bottom of the pipe, where waste sits. The inside rusts into a rough scale, and the pipe may look fine on the outside. Repair options include replacing the bad section with modern pipe or lining it when the pipe is still sound.

If a cleaning only gives temporary relief, the next step is a sewer camera inspection and a repair plan. For the cleaning itself, our sewer line cleaning page covers cable and jetting.

City sewer or septic: know which you have

The City of West Linn operates 115 miles of sewer lines, according to its public works page. If your property connects to one of them, your lateral runs to the city main. If it does not, it likely has a septic system, and the plumbing inside the house is handled the same way, while the tank and drain field are a separate specialty.

Not sure which you have? Look for a cleanout and a tank lid in the yard, or ask Public Works Operations at 503-722-5500. A camera look through sewer inspection shows what the lateral connects to.
