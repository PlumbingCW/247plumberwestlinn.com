Tankless Water Heater in West Linn, OR

A tankless water heater heats water as it flows, so there is no tank to run empty. West Linn tankless service covers installation, repair, flushing and descaling, and the phone line is open 24/7 if a unit fails.

Flushing and descaling a tankless unit

Inside a tankless unit, a heat exchanger transfers heat to the water. Minerals build up on the exchanger and restrict flow, and the unit works harder, runs hotter and eventually shows an error. A flush circulates a descaling solution through the unit and then rinses it out.

West Linn's water is soft, with hardness of 36 parts per million in the city's 2024 water quality report. That slows buildup, but it does not remove the need. Most manufacturers recommend periodic flushing, and the plumber can tell you what your model calls for. Filters and inlet screens also collect debris that has nothing to do with hardness.

Low hot water output and temperature swings

A tankless heater that gives only warm water often has one of four problems: a clogged inlet screen, scale on the exchanger, a gas supply that cannot keep up or a flow rate too low for the unit to fire. Cold sandwich effect, where hot water arrives, turns cold, then returns hot, can come from short run times at low flow.

The first step is usually the cheapest. The plumber checks the inlet screen, flow sensor and error history, then moves to the exchanger and gas supply. Low hot water pressure often turns out to be a restriction at a fixture, not the unit itself.

Tankless installation and conversions

Converting from a tank to tankless is more than swapping boxes. Gas units often need a larger gas line and a sealed vent through the wall or roof. Electric tankless units draw a lot of power and may need a service upgrade. A site visit comes before a quote.

If you are comparing options, the water heater installation page lays out tank, hybrid and tankless side by side. If a tank has already failed and you need hot water fast, water heater replacement is the quicker path.

Signs your tankless heater needs service

Do not wait for a full failure.

  • Error codes that repeat after a reset.
  • Hot water that takes longer to arrive or runs out of heat on long showers.
  • Rumbling, popping or a whining fan.
  • A burner flame that looks uneven or yellow, which needs a gas technician right away.
  • It has been years since the last flush.

What to do when a tankless heater stops heating

Do these while help is on the way.

  • Check the display for an error code and write it down.
  • Confirm the gas valve or the breaker is on.
  • Do not open the vent or the gas connection yourself.
  • If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility from outside.
  • Call (503) 974-3329 with the model and the code.
