About West Linn 24/7 Plumbing

West Linn 24/7 Plumbing is a dispatch service for homeowners in West Linn, Oregon and the nearby towns of Oregon City, Gladstone, Lake Oswego and Tualatin.

When you call (503) 974-3329, a dispatcher takes the address and the problem and sends a licensed, insured plumber from our network. The plumber explains the repair and the price before work starts.

How the service works

We run a dispatch line, not a single truck. The network includes licensed plumbers who cover the Willamette River corridor, so we can match the right person to the job, whether it is a sewer backup at night or a water heater swap on a weekday.

Because we dispatch to several contractors, we do not list a single license number, owner or crew on this site. If you want to verify a plumber, ask for the license number on arrival and check it with the state licensing body before work starts.

What you can expect

These are the standards we hold every visit to.

  • A plain explanation of the problem before any work.
  • A clear price before work starts.
  • Licensed and insured plumbers.
  • A dispatch line open 24/7, including holidays.
  • No forms to fill in. You call and talk to a person.

Why we publish local facts

Plumbing depends on local conditions: the water source, the age of the houses, the sewer system and the permit office. We use West Linn's own published pages, including its water quality report, sewer pages and building division forms, and we leave a fact out when we cannot verify it.

That is why you will see statements like "ask the City of West Linn Building Division" instead of rules from memory. Permit and code rules vary by job, and the city is the right source.

What to do while you wait for a plumber

The first few minutes decide how much damage a leak does. For a burst pipe or a failed supply line, close the main water valve, open the lowest tap to drain the line and switch off the water heater. For a sewer backup, stop using water and do not flush. For a gas water heater with a gas smell, leave the house and call the gas utility from outside before you call us.

Take photographs of any damage before you clean up. They help with an insurance claim, and a plumber can use them to understand what happened before the visit.

Services we dispatch for

The most common calls are drain cleaning, water heater repair, leak detection and sewer line cleaning. Planned work includes water heater installation, repiping and plumbing installation. Businesses can use commercial plumbing for backflow, grease trap and leak response.

The services page lists all seventeen, with a short note on when to call for each.

Why homeowners call a dispatch line

Plumbing problems rarely arrive at a convenient time. A pipe splits on a holiday, a drain backs up after a storm, a water heater fails on the coldest morning. A single shop may not have anyone free, while a dispatch line can reach several plumbers and send the nearest available one.

It also means you speak to a person who asks the right questions at the start: where the water is, what has been shut off, whether anyone is in danger. That conversation shortens the visit, because the plumber arrives knowing what to bring.

How pricing works

We do not quote one flat price for a type of job, because a clog, a leak and a water heater swap vary with access, pipe age and equipment. The plumber looks at the problem first, explains what is wrong and gives a price before work starts. If the job changes, for example when a camera shows a broken pipe, the plumber stops and explains the new scope before going further.

Emergency dispatch at night or on a holiday is priced differently from a scheduled visit, and the dispatcher can tell you how when you call.
