Emergency Garbage Disposal Repair in West Linn, OR

A garbage disposal that hums but will not turn, leaks under the sink or has stopped draining can turn a dinner cleanup into a mess. Garbage disposal repair in West Linn begins with power: switch the disposal off at the wall switch and the breaker before anyone reaches near it.

When a disposal problem is an emergency

A leaking disposal drips onto the cabinet floor and can soak the base cabinet in a day. A disposal that backs up the sink with greasy water is a drain problem too. Switch it off, never put a hand in the chamber, and place a bucket under a leak. Close the dishwasher connection if it drains through the disposal.

If the sink is full of water and the disposal will not drain, avoid running the dishwasher. The dishwasher drain line connects to the disposal or the sink trap, and it will overflow if the line is blocked.

Jams, humming and a dead disposal

A hum without grinding means the flywheel is jammed. Most units have a hex socket on the bottom for turning the flywheel by hand with the power off, and a reset button that trips on overload. If both fail to restore operation, the motor or flywheel may be seized.

A silent unit with no hum and a reset that pops back out points to an electrical issue, a failed motor or a bad switch. Repair is possible on a younger unit. On an old disposal, replacement is usually the better value.

What not to put in a disposal

These cause most service calls.

  • Fibrous scraps such as celery, corn husks and artichoke leaves.
  • Grease, oil and fat, which coat the drain and harden.
  • Coffee grounds, eggshells and starchy foods in large amounts.
  • Bones, fruit pits and hard objects.
  • Anything that is not food, including silverware and bottle caps.

Garbage disposal installation

A new installation needs a sink opening, an electrical connection and a drain connection. If the kitchen has a dishwasher, the plumber also connects the dishwasher drain and knocks out the plug in the disposal inlet. Power usually comes from a switched outlet under the sink.

Horsepower and feed type matter. A continuous feed unit runs while you push scraps through. A batch feed unit runs only when a cover is in place. More horsepower grinds better and runs quieter, and a good insulation package cuts noise.

Kitchens, remodels and disposals in West Linn

Many West Linn homes from the 1950s and 1960s have kitchens that have been remodeled at least once. The Bolton historic resource survey counted 130 properties from the 1960s and 120 from the 1950s. A remodel can leave a disposal connected to an old trap arm or an undersized drain, and the sink then drains slowly even with a new unit.

If the disposal itself is fine but the sink backs up, the cause is usually the branch line, and the plumber clears it with a cable. Our drain cleaning page covers that work.
