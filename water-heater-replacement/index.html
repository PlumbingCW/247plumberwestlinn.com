Emergency Water Heater Replacement in West Linn, OR

When a tank has failed and water is on the floor, replacement is the fix. Water heater replacement in West Linn starts with stopping the water: close the cold supply valve on top of the heater, shut off power or gas, and call (503) 974-3329.

When a failed water heater is an emergency

A tank that is leaking from the body cannot be repaired because the steel liner has corroded through. Other signs include rust-colored hot water, repeated element or burner failures, rumbling that returns after flushing and a tank that is well past ten years old.

If it is leaking now, treat it as an emergency. Shut the supply, drain what you can through the hose bib if the floor allows and call. If the leak is slow and the floor is dry, you have a few days to compare options, but not weeks.

What to clear before the plumber arrives

Replacement goes faster when the path is open.

  • Clear a three-foot path to the heater and remove stored items around it.
  • Shut off the cold supply, and switch off power or gas.
  • Take a photo of the old unit label, which shows capacity, fuel and model.
  • Note any odd venting, pipe routing or relief line that looks repaired.

Choosing the right replacement

Most homes replace like with like: a 40 or 50 gallon tank to a 40 or 50 gallon tank. That avoids new venting and electrical work and keeps the job quick. If your household has grown, or the old tank ran out of hot water, a larger tank or a different type may be worth the added cost.

Options include standard tank, hybrid heat pump tank and tankless. A hybrid uses less electricity but needs clearance and a condensate drain. Tankless saves space but needs correct gas or electric capacity and venting.

What a replacement includes

The old tank is drained, disconnected and hauled out. The new unit is set, connected with new flexible connectors, and fitted with a new relief valve and discharge line. Expansion tank, seismic strapping and venting are checked and corrected where the code requires them.
