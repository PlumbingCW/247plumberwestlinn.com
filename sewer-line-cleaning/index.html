Sewer backup: what to do first

A sewer backup shows in the lowest fixtures first: a basement floor drain, a ground-floor tub or a toilet. Stop running water, including the washing machine and dishwasher. Do not flush. Keep the area clear of children and pets, and avoid touching the water.

If sewage is overflowing into a finished area, protect floors where you can and open windows. The plumber starts at the cleanout and works toward the blockage, so clear access to it.

Find the cleanout before the plumber arrives

A reachable cleanout saves time and cost.

  • Look for a capped pipe near the foundation, usually four inches wide, in a yard, basement or crawlspace.
  • Clear shrubs, stored items and snow from around it.
  • Do not open the cap if sewage is under pressure behind it.
  • Note whether the problem started with one fixture or several.

Cable machines, jetters and what we use

A cable machine feeds a rotating steel cable with a cutting head into the line, and it cuts through roots and breaks up blockages. A hydro jetter uses high pressure water to scour the pipe wall. Which one the plumber chooses depends on the blockage, the pipe material and what the cleanout allows.

Heavy roots and compacted debris often call for a cable first, then a jetter to finish. When a camera confirms the line is intact, the hydro jetting pass removes scale that would otherwise let the clog return.

After the line is clear

A cleaned line is not always a fixed line. A sewer camera inspection right after the cleaning shows the pipe walls, joints and any damage. It also gives you a recording to keep and share with a repair contractor or insurer.

If the cleaning was routine maintenance on a line with a known root problem, a scheduled visit once or twice a year can prevent emergency calls. If the line is sound and the cause was a one-time event, no follow-up may be needed.

West Linn runs along the Willamette River, and Oregon's wet season brings heavy rain that can load sewer systems. If a backup happens during a storm and the whole street is affected, the cause may be the public main rather than your pipe. The plumber can check with a camera at the cleanout, and the City of West Linn public works office is the contact for city main problems.
