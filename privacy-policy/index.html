Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we collect when you visit this site or call (503) 974-3329, and how we use it.

Call (503) 974-3329

Calls

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call we may collect your phone number, address and the details you give about your plumbing problem, so that we can send a plumber.

Website information

This site has no forms. We do not ask you to enter personal details on the site. Like most websites, our hosting provider and any analytics tools may collect technical information such as your browser, device and pages visited.

How information is used

We use call information to dispatch a plumber, to improve service and to follow up on a job. We share the address and problem details with the plumber who is sent to your home. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call. To ask about information we hold, email contact@247plumberwestlinn.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page.
