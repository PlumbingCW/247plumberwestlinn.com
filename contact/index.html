Contact West Linn 24/7 Plumbing

What to have ready when you call

Have the address, what is happening and what you have already done, such as closing the main valve. If you can, say whether the problem is one fixture or the whole house. The dispatcher uses that to send the right plumber and equipment.

For an active leak, close the main water valve first. For a sewer backup, stop using water. Then call. Our services page lists everything we handle.
